Read Me
  1. Download Crossway.zip and unzip it (double-click).
  2. Drag Crossway.app into your /Applications folder.
  3. Open it. Crossway lives in the menu bar, so you won't see a dock icon.
  4. Grant the two permissions it asks for in System Settings → Privacy & Security: Accessibility (to take over ⌘Tab and raise the window you pick) and Screen Recording (for the window previews).
  5. Choose the final Startup options. Launch at login starts on; Auto-update stays off unless you choose it. Once both permissions are granted, Crossway finishes setting up on its own.

Always download in a web browser, from this page. Files sent through chat apps get marked by macOS and won't open. If that happens, the fix is on the Troubleshooting page.

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, including Sequoia and Tahoe. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Privacy

Crossway records nothing and uploads nothing.

Nothing is saved to disk except your settings. Window previews live in memory only and are never written anywhere.

Auto-update is optional and opt-in, off by default, and checks only on the schedule you choose. Crossway fetches release metadata from GitHub; it never downloads or installs an update itself.

The same metadata-only check is always available manually from Check for Updates in the menu bar or Check now in Settings. There is no telemetry, and there are no identifiers or uploads.

Every release is signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple. An available update offers a deliberate browser download; Crossway never replaces the app unattended. You can replace it manually, or separately invoke the external skill above and confirm its verified replacement plan.
