Interactive demo

Use your cursor to click, drag, and interact with the windows, dock, and more!

Click the keys with your mouse.

Step 1 click to hold
Step 2 click to tap
  1. ⌘⇥ Switch apps See every window before you land on it.
  2. ⌘` Switch windows Go straight to the window you want. No trackpad.
  3. ⌥⇥ All windows Everything open, on one grid.
  4. ⌥` App windows Only this app's windows, on one grid.

This is just a first taste. Crossway has many more settings and features for you to explore and customize.
