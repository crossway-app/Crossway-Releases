Read Me
  1. Download Crossway.zip and unzip it (double-click).
  2. Drag Crossway.app into your /Applications folder.
  3. Open it. Crossway lives in the menu bar, so you won't see a dock icon.
  4. Grant the two permissions it asks for in System Settings → Privacy & Security: Accessibility (to take over ⌘Tab and raise the window you pick) and Screen Recording (for the window previews).
  5. Choose the final Startup options. Launch at login starts on; Auto-update stays off unless you choose it. Once both permissions are granted, Crossway finishes setting up on its own.

Files sent through chat apps get marked by macOS and won't open. If that happens, the fix is on the Troubleshooting page.

Requires macOS 14 to 26. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Privacy

Crossway doesn't record or upload anything.

Nothing is saved to disk except your settings. Window previews live in memory only. There is no telemetry, and Crossway sends no identifiers.

Crossway only goes online to check GitHub for new versions. It checks when you ask it to, or on its own if you turn on Auto-update.

Every release is signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple.

Features

Crossway has many features, like ⌘W to close the selected window and ⌘Q to quit an app, right from the app switcher. See all features.
